About the practice

A small, deliberate consultancy — one engineer, deep involvement.

I've spent eight years building mobile products inside startups — the kind where you're the developer, the architect, the code reviewer, and the person explaining tradeoffs to non-technical founders all in the same week.

Apps I've shipped span travel eSIM, fintech payroll, government civic, real estate, and cross-cultural social. I've led teams, mentored juniors into capable mid-level engineers, and sat on both sides of the interview table for senior global roles.

Writing code is easy. Making the right technical decisions is what defines a senior engineer.

The work is practical, not theoretical. Scalable, not clever. Business-aware, not buzzword-driven. Every engagement begins with a real problem and ends with a decision you can act on.
